Pregnancy care: when should you see a gynecologist?

Book your first visit as soon as a home pregnancy test is positive, ideally by 6–8 weeks. The first scan confirms the pregnancy is inside the uterus and shows the heartbeat. After that, visits are monthly until 28 weeks, every two weeks until 36 weeks, and weekly until delivery.

StageWhat happens at this visit
6–8 weeksBooking visit, dating scan, blood group, haemoglobin, thyroid, sugar and infection screening

24–28 weeksGlucose test for gestational diabetes, tetanus dose, iron and calcium review

Anaemia deserves a special mention. It is extremely common in this region and is one of the most preventable causes of complications during delivery. Iron and folic acid supplementation, started early and taken consistently, changes outcomes far more than most patients realise.

Normal delivery or caesarean — which hospital is best for delivery in Ambikapur?

Any hospital you choose for delivery should have three things available at all hours: a doctor who can perform an emergency caesarean, blood arrangement, and newborn care support. Ask about these directly. The answer tells you more than the size of the waiting room.

Normal delivery is always preferred when it is safe for mother and baby. What genuinely improves the chances: regular antenatal visits, controlled weight gain, correcting anaemia early, staying active through pregnancy, and monitoring the baby's position after 36 weeks. A caesarean is advised when there is a real medical reason — foetal distress, obstructed labour, placenta praevia, a breech baby, or certain previous surgeries.

On cost: a normal delivery in a private setup in Ambikapur is usually considerably cheaper than a caesarean, since a caesarean adds theatre charges, anaesthesia and a longer stay. Government facilities cost less; private care generally offers continuity with the same doctor from the first visit to discharge. Either way, ask for a written estimate.

PCOS, irregular periods and the symptoms women delay too long

Can PCOS be treated without surgery?

Yes — in most cases. PCOS is a hormonal and metabolic condition, so the foundation of treatment is lifestyle: losing even 5–10% of body weight can restore ovulation on its own. Medicines are added to regularise cycles, improve insulin sensitivity, or induce ovulation when a woman is trying to conceive. Laparoscopic ovarian drilling is reserved for the small group who don't respond to medical treatment.

What causes irregular periods after 30?

The usual causes are PCOS, thyroid disorders, anaemia, fibroids or polyps, sudden weight change, and stress. After 40, perimenopause enters the picture. A pelvic ultrasound along with thyroid, haemoglobin and hormone tests usually identifies the reason within a single visit — which is why "waiting for it to settle on its own" for a year rarely helps.

Symptoms that should not wait

  • Bleeding between periods, after intercourse, or any bleeding after menopause
  • Periods lasting more than seven days, or soaking through protection every one to two hours
  • Severe pelvic pain that stops you from working or attending school
  • Not conceiving after one year of trying — six months if you are over 35
  • A breast lump, or persistent foul-smelling discharge
