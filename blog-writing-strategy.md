# Blog Writing Strategy for Dr. Ankita Goyal Bansal

## Core Guidelines

### Blog Title
- Keep attractive and clear
- Include main keyword
- Keep under 60 characters if possible

### Introduction
- Mention main keyword within first 100 words
- Clearly explain patient's problem
- Tell readers what they will learn

### Headings Structure
- Only ONE H1 (main title)
- H2 for main sections
- H3 for sub-sections
- Keep headings simple and patient-friendly

### Content Quality
- Write in simple Hindi
- Avoid complex medical jargon
- Answer common patient concerns
- Provide accurate medical information
- Focus on patient education

## Important Sections to Include
1. What is the condition?
2. Symptoms
3. Causes
4. When surgery is needed
5. How laparoscopic surgery is performed
6. Benefits of laparoscopic surgery
7. Risks and complications
8. Recovery process
9. FAQs

### Local SEO
- Mention city naturally (Ambikapur, Chhattisgarh)
- Mention clinic/hospital name (Sankalp Hospital)
- Include location-specific keywords

### Images
- Add relevant medical illustrations/images
- Use descriptive image names
- Add ALT text to every image

### Internal Linking
- Link to related blog posts
- Link to treatment pages
- Link to doctor profile pages
- Link to contact/appointment pages

### Meta Tags
- SEO-friendly Meta Title (under 60 chars)
- Meta Description (150-160 chars)
- Include primary keyword in both

### E-E-A-T (Medical SEO)
- Doctor's name: Dr. Ankita Bansal Goyal
- Qualifications: Experienced Gynecologist & Laparoscopic Surgeon
- Experience: Years of experience in laparoscopic surgeries
- Hospital: Sankalp Hospital, Ambikapur
- State that content is medically reviewed

### FAQ Section
- 5-10 patient questions
- Real questions patients commonly ask
- Concise answers

### Call to Action (CTA)
- Encourage appointment booking
- Provide contact details
- Invite patients to ask questions

### Before Publishing Checklist
- [ ] Check spelling and grammar
- [ ] Ensure mobile-friendly formatting
- [ ] Verify medical accuracy
- [ ] Make sure all links work
- [ ] Check keyword placement
- [ ] Add schema markup (FAQ schema if possible)

### Ideal Blog Length
- Minimum: 800-1,000 words
- Best: 1,200-1,800 words
- Comprehensive guides: 2,000+ words

## Blog Post Structure Template

```markdown
# H1 Blog Title (Under 60 chars, includes main keyword)

## Introduction (100-150 words)
- Main keyword in first 100 words
- Patient problem explanation
- What readers will learn

## What is [Condition]? (H2)
- Simple explanation
- Easy to understand language
- Avoid medical jargon

## Symptoms (H2)
- List common symptoms
- When to worry
- When to see doctor

## Causes (H2)
- What causes this condition
- Risk factors
- Prevention tips

## When Surgery is Needed (H2)
- When conservative treatment fails
- Indications for surgery
- Why laparoscopic approach

## How Laparoscopic Surgery is Performed (H2)
- Step-by-step explanation
- What to expect
- Duration of procedure

## Benefits of Laparoscopic Surgery (H2)
- Less pain
- Faster recovery
- Smaller scars
- Shorter hospital stay
- Quick return to normal activities

## Risks and Complications (H2)
- Common risks
- Rare complications
- How to minimize risks

## Recovery Process (H2)
- Immediate post-op care
- Home care instructions
- Follow-up appointments
- When to resume normal activities

## FAQs (H2)
- 5-10 common questions
- Patient-friendly answers
- Real patient concerns

## Call to Action (H2)
- Book appointment
- Contact details
- Hospital location
- Doctor profile link

## Meta Description
- 150-160 characters
- Primary keyword included
- Compelling to click
```
